How it works

Four steps, no middleman.

01

Post what you have or need

Pick your course, section, term and campus, and say whether you're dropping it or looking for a seat.

02

Get grouped automatically

Your post joins everyone else's for that exact course, so anyone looking for what you have can find you.

03

Message each other

Sign in with your YorkU email to chat directly with the other student and work out the details.

04

Swap it yourselves

We only make the connection — you each make the actual change in your own Student Center.
